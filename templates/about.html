What is TF-IDF?

TF-IDF (Term Frequency – Inverse Document Frequency) converts text into numbers a model can learn from. Each word's weight combines:

  • Term Frequency: how often the word appears in this message.
  • Inverse Document Frequency: how rare it is across all messages, so ubiquitous words like "the" are down-weighted and distinctive words like "prize" are boosted.

Each message becomes a {{ "{:,}".format(metrics.vectorizer.vocab_size) }}-dimensional vector over unigrams and bigrams {{ metrics.vectorizer.ngram_range }}. We also benchmarked a raw-count CountVectorizer:

Why {{ best_model }}?

A Linear Support Vector Machine finds the hyperplane that best separates spam from ham in the high-dimensional TF-IDF space, maximising the margin between classes. It's ideal for text because:

  • It thrives in high-dimensional, sparse feature spaces, which is exactly what bag-of-words produces.
  • It's fast to train and resistant to overfitting.
  • Its linear weights are interpretable, so we read them directly to surface the trigger tokens behind every prediction.

Since a raw SVM only outputs a decision score, we wrap it in probability calibration so the app can report a meaningful confidence percentage.
